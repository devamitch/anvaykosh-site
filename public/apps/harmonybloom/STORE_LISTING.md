HarmonyBloom is a mindful sanctuary for women's hormonal harmony, natural cycle awareness, and circadian peace.

Featuring a serene porcelain, terracotta, and pista sanctuary aesthetic, HarmonyBloom provides a respectful, science-backed sanctuary to understand your body's natural rhythms, observe vitality trends, log daily symptoms, and restore inner calm.

✨ HOLISTIC SANCTUARY CAPABILITIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🌸 MINDFUL CYCLE RHYTHM DIAL
• Natural Phase Awareness: Visualize follicular, ovulatory, luteal, and menstrual phases on an intuitive circular dial.
• Physiological Vitality Rhythms: Gentle insights into energy fluctuations, mood shifts, and metabolic rhythms.
• Zero Guesswork Predictions: Clearly indicates uncertainty bounds without making ungrounded medical claims.

🌿 HOLISTIC SYMPTOM & MOOD LOGGING
• Sensitive Factor Logging: Easily track bleeding intensity, cramps, mood, sleep quality, and physical comfort.
• Custom Notes: Maintain a private, encrypted personal journal of daily sensations and nutrition habits.
• Historical Trends: Scannable monthly charts illustrate personal hormonal patterns over time.

🌙 CIRCADIAN PEACE & EVENING REST
• Restorative Sanctuary: Evening wind-down prompts and calming visual themes to support restorative sleep.
• Mindful Breathing: Guided slow-breathing exercises tailored to reduce cycle tension and soothe stress.

🔒 NON-NEGOTIABLE SENSITIVE DATA ISOLATION
• Total Enclave Privacy: Reproductive, cycle, and intimate health records remain strictly isolated on your device.
• Zero Cross-App Sharing: Sibling ecosystem apps cannot access your cycle logs without explicit, granular, informed opt-in.
• No Third-Party Trackers: Zero advertising SDKs, zero third-party analytics, and no cloud data selling.

✨ ANVAYKOSH ECOSYSTEM INTEGRATION
• Anvay ID: Sovereign 1-tap authentication connecting your profile basics without exposing health data.
• Offline-First Engine: Full offline logging and calendar functionality in Airplane Mode.
