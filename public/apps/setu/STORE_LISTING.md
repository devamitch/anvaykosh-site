### Full Description (1384/4000 chars)
```text
Setu is a local-first, infrastructure-independent cooperative content network for nearby devices.

Whether you are off the grid, traveling, in crowded spaces, or simply value your digital sovereignty, Setu allows you to discover nearby devices and share content securely without relying on cloud servers or internet connectivity.

KEY FEATURES:
• Zero Cloud Infrastructure: Direct peer-to-peer file transfers and messaging over Wi-Fi Direct and local networks.
• High-Speed Swarm Transfer: Transfer large videos, photos, documents, and files at the maximum speed of your device's Wi-Fi hardware.
• Cryptographic Security: Local device-generated keys ensure authenticated, encrypted sessions with trusted peers.
• Private Vault: Protect sensitive media and documents on your device with biometric authentication.
• Audio & Media Player: Built-in native media player with playback for received music and videos.
• Offline Emergency Sharing: Broadcast critical messages or alerts to nearby peers even when mobile networks are unavailable.
• Privacy First: No accounts, no phone numbers, no tracking, and zero ads. Your data belongs only to you.

THE ANVAYKOSH ECOSYSTEM:
Setu serves as the flagship identity and encrypted vault anchor for the sovereign AnvayKosh ecosystem, enabling peer-to-peer authorization and cross-app communication across all registered Amit Dev Labs applications.
```
